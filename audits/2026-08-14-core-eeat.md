## Top five weighted improvements

1. **T02 Contact Transparency** — after repository creation, add the working public issue URL to both trust blocks. Potential normalized gain: about 1.7 points.
2. **T03 Security Standards** — verify live HTTPS, console state and no network submission after deployment. Potential normalized gain: about 1.7 points.
3. **T05 Editorial Policy** — bind the visible review method to the approved reviewer and exact versions without adding a third Pharos anchor. Potential normalized gain: about 1.7 points.
4. **C09 FAQ Coverage** — keep at Partial unless a genuinely new long-tail question appears; duplicating body answers would reduce GIST quality despite a mechanical score gain.
5. **Ept01/Ept02 Author Identity and Credentials** — add the real reviewer, role, verifiable credential/profile and timestamp only after explicit approval. Potential normalized gain: about 1.3 combined points.

## Release conclusion

The page clears all three veto items and has no Referenceability or Trust Fail. It is not publication-ready because the named technical review required by its own contract is still pending. Approval must update the exact scoped review record; it must not be inferred from this audit.
